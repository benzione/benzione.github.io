Privacy policy

The developer does not collect personal data from Jewish Calendar M. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Reminder titles, dates, alert settings, and calendar preferences are stored on your device. If you enable alerts, iOS delivers them locally. Delete a reminder in the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove its local data.

Starting in version 1.2, optional Google sign-in identifies your Google account so the app can save your reminders, deletion records, and holiday preferences to its private app data folder in your Google Drive. If you also connect Google Calendar, the app publishes reminders and Jewish events to a separate calendar it creates in your account. Google processes the data according to your Google account settings. The app does not use a separate server to store your reminders. The app keeps a device copy of account reminders to support offline use, including pending changes.

You can sign out from the app. To remove cloud copies, remove the calendar in Google Calendar and manage the app's stored data in your Google account.

In version 1.1, if you enable calendar sync, the app uses iPhone Calendar access to identify the selected calendar and check it for existing copies. It copies reminder titles, dates, times, and alert settings into the calendar you select. The account provider, such as Google, then syncs that calendar under its own privacy policy. Anyone with whom you share the destination calendar can see the copied entries.

You can disconnect sync in the app or revoke Calendar access in iPhone Settings. Disconnecting stops new updates. You can remove existing copies in the destination calendar.

Calendar data is included in the app. If you choose to open a source or license link in Settings, that external website handles your visit under its own privacy policy.
