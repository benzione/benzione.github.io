Jewish Calendar M

Support for the Hebrew calendar and reminders app for iPhone.

Change the holiday schedule

Open Settings in the app and choose Israel or Diaspora under Holiday schedule. Holiday and Torah reading data from Hebcal is available offline for 2010–2069.

Add a reminder

Choose a date in Calendar and tap the plus button. Choose the repeat schedule and optionally enable an alert. You can edit or delete the entry in Reminders.

Check your alerts

Allow notifications in iPhone Settings, and open the app periodically to refresh upcoming dated alerts. Dates use the daytime civil date and do not account for local sunset.

Google account and Calendar sync

In version 1.2, open Settings → Google account and sign in. Your personal reminders and holiday schedule settings are saved in your own Google Drive. You can copy existing reminders from this iPhone after signing in. Tap Connect Google Calendar to show Jewish events and personal reminders in a separate calendar under the same Google account.

Sign out from the same screen to return to the local reminders on this device. Sign in again with the same Google account to restore your cloud reminders. Make edits in Jewish Calendar M and open the app to sync changes.

Calendar sync in version 1.1

In version 1.1, create a private calendar in Google Calendar on the web. On your iPhone, add the same Google account under Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts and turn on Calendars. In Jewish Calendar M, go to Settings → Google Calendar sync, allow Calendar access, select the private calendar, and tap Start sync.

Sync copies personal reminders, holidays, Shabbat, and Torah readings. It includes one year of history and five years ahead, plus the original date of every saved reminder. Open the app periodically to extend the future dates. Make edits in Jewish Calendar M and let iPhone Calendar send the updates to Google.
